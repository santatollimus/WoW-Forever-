SparkleButt v0.6.3 - Arrival Glow + Snack Personality (experimental)

Installation: Fully exit WoW. Back up 0.6.2, then replace ONLY the SparkleButt
addon folder. Keep your working Starfall Trail 1.2.0 and bridge untouched.

NEW: Pet mode keeps its emerald glow after Starfall confirms destination arrival,
regardless of facing. Auto mode inherits that glow while resting beside you.
A new active tracked route clears the arrival celebration and restores normal
heading-based glow. Offscreen visibility never counts as arrival.

NEW: /sb snack now has varied arrival, eating, and return dialogue; the lines
change with repeated snacks (eager, spoiled, stuffed) and the streak resets
if you haven't offered a snack for 10 minutes. Hearts and visual treat stay.
No inventory item is consumed. This is a UI/model effect, not a spawned creature.

Chase, Pet left/right, Auto flight transitions, map waypoints, and the four-dot
selector are otherwise unchanged. In-game validation is required.
