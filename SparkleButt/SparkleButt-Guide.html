Meet SparkleButt.

An emerald dragon companion for navigation, mouseover reconnaissance, and small moments of personality. Designed to make exploring Azeroth easier without demanding constant target-clicking or minimap watching.

What SparkleButt does

🧭 Follows your route

Works with a compatible StarfallTrail installation to guide you toward route destinations. Auto can transition between guiding and resting beside your character; Chase is the active guidance mode.

🔎 SparkleRadar

Hover over a creature or player without clicking. Radar prints readable names, levels, creature types, and player classes in chat, using reaction and class colors. Individual unit GUIDs help suppress repeated announcements.

🟡 Quest objective hints

A filled yellow dot before a creature's name indicates that its readable mouseover tooltip contains an unfinished quest objective. Completed objective sections are excluded.

✨ Rare & elite alerts

Special sparkle-and-dragon chat announcements are intended for rare and elite units detected on mouseover. The rare alert still needs a confirmed live rare-spawn test.

📏 Target range bands

Show an estimate above or below the dragon when it is resting beside you. Examples: 10 yd, 20 yd, 30 yd, and > 30 yd. These are thresholds, not exact distance measurements.

💚 An actual companion

Pet mode can sit on either side of your character. Smooth flight transitions help the dragon move between jobs. Give it a pretend snack to see hearts and personality dialogue.

The six controls

Auto

Automatically switches between guiding and resting according to route status.

Chase

Follow the current navigation direction.

Pet

Stay beside you. Selecting Pet again switches sides.

Target Range

Cycle the range label: above, below, or off.

SparkleRadar

Orange means Radar enabled; gray means disabled. This toggle is independent of navigation.

Off

Turn off both the dragon and Radar. Restarting a navigation mode does not automatically re-enable Radar.

Install & first flight

Download the SparkleButt source folder from this repository, and obtain StarfallTrail from its original author. SparkleButt requires StarfallTrail and our integration changes.
Place both addon folders under your WoW Forever Interface/AddOns directory. Ensure the integration changes have been applied to the compatible StarfallTrail version.
Start or reload WoW with /reload. Open the addon selector or type /sb auto to begin.
Try /sb radar on and hover over a creature; then try /sb snack.

The integration changes are supplied separately from StarfallTrail's original distribution. Compatibility with later upstream StarfallTrail releases has not yet been confirmed.

What to expect (and not expect)

Not an automatic rare scanner. Radar inspects units you actually hover over. It does not discover unseen rares, independently track distant gathering nodes, or read protected minimap marker information.

The current public-ready feature set is built around unit mouseover. Herb, mining, and chest identification were experimented with but were removed from the release due to incorrect duplicate reports. Target range is based on available range-check thresholds, not an exact yard-by-yard API. Displayed quest hints depend on readable tooltip data and may not work with every tooltip configuration.
